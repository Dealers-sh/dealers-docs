The Dealers.sh contracts are currently in audit. We are not shipping mainnet until the audit is signed off and findings have been remediated.

## Scope

The audit covers every contract listed in [/contracts/overview](/contracts/overview).

## Report

The signed report will be linked here when it lands. It will be hosted publicly. We will not publish a summary in lieu of the report.

## Remediation log

Each finding will be tracked from "open" to "remediated" with a commit hash. The log will appear here.

## Why we wait

We'd rather ship a week later than ship something that bricks holders. The character art and game live in the bytecode. A bad upgrade is a permanent scar.
