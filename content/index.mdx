# Welcome to the family.

Dealers.sh is a 100% on-chain mafia strategy game powered by dynamic NFTs.
Buy low, sell high. Watch the heat. Make rep. Stay out of jail.

It's not a roadmap, it's a contract. Read it.

## Three doors

[`/start`](/start/connect) puts you in. Connect a wallet, learn what your dealer is, run your first deal in five minutes.

[`/the-game`](/the-game/overview) is every tab in the game, explained. PVE, PVP, Move, Heist, Stash, Shop, Pager, Jail. The mechanics, no fluff.

[`/contracts`](/contracts/overview) is the architecture. Verified addresses on Abstract. The bytecode is the truth. If something we wrote here disagrees with the contract, the contract wins.

## The pitch, deadpan

No servers. No storage. No hosting. Powered and rendered on Abstract.

The character art is rendered from the contract. The game runs inside the NFT's `animation_url`. Your dealer levels up. Your JPEG doesn't.

PVE for the rep. PVP for the infamy. Heists for the bag.

## What this site is

This is the manual. The game itself does not stop and explain itself. If a button confused you, the answer is in here.

If you want the marketing front page instead, go to [dealers.sh](https://dealers.sh).
