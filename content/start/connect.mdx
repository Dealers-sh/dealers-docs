# /start/connect

Dealers.sh runs on Abstract. To play, you need a wallet that signs on Abstract.

## Option 1. Abstract Global Wallet

AGW is the easiest path in. Sign up with an email or a social account. No seed phrase to lose. Bridge in funds, hit Connect inside the NFT viewer, and the dealer is yours.

[Create an Abstract Global Wallet →](https://abs.xyz)

## Option 2. Injected wallet

Any wallet that can sign on Abstract works. MetaMask, Rabby, Frame, anything with the Abstract chain added. Hit Connect inside the NFT viewer, sign, you're in.

## Where you actually play

The game is embedded inside the NFT. You play by opening your Dealers.sh NFT in any viewer that respects `animation_url`:

- The Abstract explorer
- Magic Eden Abstract
- Any marketplace that renders animated NFTs
- Your own dApp

No separate app to download. No login screen on a website. The NFT is the game.
