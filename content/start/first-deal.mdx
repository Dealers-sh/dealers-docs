# /start/first-deal

This page walks you through your very first session. It assumes you have a connected wallet and a dealer NFT in your possession. If you do not yet, see [/start/connect](/start/connect).

## 1. Open your dealer

Open your Dealers.sh NFT in any marketplace or explorer that renders the `animation_url` field. The character art appears in the background, and the game UI loads in front of it. The whole experience runs inside the NFT viewer itself.

## 2. Accept the terms

The very first time you open a dealer, the game prompts you to accept the terms of service and the privacy policy. Both are short, both are linked in full from the prompt, and the acceptance is recorded against your wallet so you only see it once. You cannot take any in-game action until you accept.

## 3. Set up a session for fast play

After you accept the terms, the game offers to set up a session. A session is a short-lived signer that the game uses on your behalf for ordinary in-game actions, so that buying, selling, moving, and fighting no longer pop a wallet confirmation for every single transaction. You sign once to authorise the session, and from that point on the game plays at full speed until the session expires. Sensitive actions like withdrawing cash or transferring the NFT still require a direct wallet confirmation.

Setting up a session is optional, but strongly recommended. Without one, every action triggers a wallet popup, which makes the game noticeably slower.

## 4. Run the onboarding

If this is a brand new dealer that has never taken an action before, the game launches a short onboarding the first time you open it. The onboarding walks you through the basic mechanics: the tabs along the side, the meaning of each stat, how to read prices, how a deal resolves, and how heat and reputation move. It takes a couple of minutes and is designed to give you just enough context to start playing without spoiling the deeper systems.

You can leave the onboarding at any time and you can restart it later from the 📟 [pager](/the-game/pager) tab by typing the `/tutorial` command. Returning players who skipped the onboarding the first time around can use the same command to catch up.

## 5. Read the stash

The STASH tab is the default view and acts as your dashboard. The left half shows your stats: reputation, heat, cash, attempts, and the area you are currently in. The right half lists your current inventory of drugs by type and quantity. At the very start of the game, the inventory is empty and your reputation is zero. That is normal.

## 6. Tap the deal tab

The 🤝 icon opens the deal tab. While you are on the streets of any normal area, you will see three buttons: Buy, Sell, and Clear Heat. For your first move, choose Buy. The game then asks you which drug you want, how much you want, and which strategy to use during the negotiation. Pick anything reasonable and confirm the transaction.

## 7. Read the result

When the transaction is mined, the chat log inside the game writes back what happened. You will see how much cash changed hands, whether the deal succeeded, how much reputation you earned, and how much heat the encounter generated. A small amount of heat after a single deal is expected.

## 8. Travel and sell

Each area in Dealers.sh has its own price list. The drug you just bought will usually be cheaper in some areas and more expensive in others. Open the ✈️ tab, scroll through the nearby areas, choose one with a better price for what you are carrying, and confirm the move. Once you land, open the deal tab again and sell the drug at its new price.

That single round trip is the core loop of the game in miniature. You buy where prices are low, you move when prices change, and you sell where prices are high. Reputation accrues as you do it, your heat rises along the way, and the more careful you are with both, the further you go.

When you are ready for the full mechanics, the next stop is [/the-game/overview](/the-game/overview).
