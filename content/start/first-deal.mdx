# /start/first-deal

Five minutes. Here's the loop.

## 1. Open your dealer

Load your Dealers.sh NFT. The game loads inside it. The character art sits behind the UI.

## 2. STASH is your dashboard

The STASH tab shows your stats on the left and your inventory on the right. That's the dashboard. Open it when you want to see where you stand.

## 3. Tap 🤝 to make a deal

The 🤝 tab is PVE. You get three options on the streets:

- **BUY** the local drug at the local price.
- **SELL** something from your stash.
- **CLEAR HEAT** if you've got heat to clean.

For your first move: BUY. Pick a drug. Pick a small amount. Pick a strategy. Hit confirm.

## 4. Watch the result

The deal resolves on chain. The chat log tells you what happened: how much you paid, what the result was, what changed.

You probably picked up a little heat. That's normal.

## 5. Move when prices shift

✈️ shows you nearby areas. Prices vary by area. Different drugs in different places. Travel to a richer market, sell what you bought.

That's the loop. Buy low. Sell high. Watch the heat. Make rep.

Welcome to the family.
