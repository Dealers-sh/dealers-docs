# /brand-assets

The Dealers.sh brand book lives in Figma. It holds the logo, wordmark, color palette, type, and the rules for putting them together.

- **Brand book:** [Dealers.sh Brand Book on Figma](https://www.figma.com/design/iesP9GDDyO4Zc9FFwAlDjv/Dealers-Brand-Book?node-id=0-1&t=tRHg5kfJ4SGplOZg-1)
