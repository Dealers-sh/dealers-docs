# /the-art/rarity

Every dealer carries a fixed visual trait set that is encoded on chain when the NFT mints. The trait categories cover the dealer's body, outfit, accessories, and the surrounding scene — together they produce the silhouette you see when you open the token. Rarity is simply the result of how often each trait, and each combination of traits, appears across the collection.

## Rarity is readable on chain

The trait assignments live inside `DealersNFT`, and the SVG renderer `DealerRendererSVG` composes them at call time. No middleware or off-chain index is required: anyone with an Abstract RPC connection can read the trait data directly.

## Rarity is cosmetic

Rarity affects how your dealer looks. It does not affect any gameplay system. Reputation, infamy, heat, and cash are independent of which traits you rolled, and a common dealer with a high reputation total outranks a one-of-one with no track record.

For aggregate rarity views — sorted trait counts, ranking, filters — third-party marketplaces and explorers index the on-chain trait data after mint. The docs do not maintain rarity tables.
