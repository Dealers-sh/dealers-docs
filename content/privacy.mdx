## 1. Scope

This policy covers data handling for the Dealers.sh marketing site, this documentation site, and any first-party interfaces operated by us. It does not cover Abstract, your wallet provider, or any third-party block explorer or marketplace.

## 2. What we collect

**On the websites:**

- Server logs (IP address, user agent, referrer, timestamp).
- Cookies for theme preference and basic analytics.
- Optional, only if you submit something: email address.

**On chain:**

- Your wallet address is public.
- Every action you take in the game is a public on-chain event.
- We do not link your wallet to off-chain identifiers unless you connect them yourself.

## 3. What we don't collect

- We do not run KYC.
- We do not request personal identification documents.
- We do not sell user data. There is nothing to sell.

## 5. Third parties

The Service interacts with:

- Abstract chain infrastructure (your wallet provider, RPC providers).
- Marketplaces that render the NFT.
- Vercel (or equivalent) for hosting.

These third parties have their own policies. We do not share data with them beyond what's necessary to render the site.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete data we hold. Because we hold very little off-chain data, most requests will be quick.

On-chain data is, by definition, not deletable.

## 7. Security

We follow standard practices for the small amount of off-chain data we hold. No system is perfect. If you suspect a compromise, contact us.

## 8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the local equivalent age).
