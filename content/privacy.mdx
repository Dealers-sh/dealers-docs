# /privacy

**Last updated:** to be set before launch.

This page is a working draft of the Dealers.sh privacy policy. It will be finalised in collaboration with counsel ahead of mainnet launch. Please do not treat the language below as legally reviewed in its current form.

## 1. Scope

This policy describes how Dealers.sh handles personal data on the marketing site at dealers.sh, on this documentation site, and on any other first-party interface operated by the project. It does not cover the data practices of Abstract itself, your wallet provider, any third-party block explorer, or any marketplace that renders the Dealers.sh NFT through its own interface. Those parties have their own privacy policies.

## 2. What we collect

On our websites, the data we receive falls into two categories. Routine server logs include the IP address, user agent, referrer, and timestamp of each request, which is standard for any site hosted on the open internet. Cookies are used to remember your theme preference (dark or light) and for a small amount of aggregate analytics. If you submit information to us voluntarily, for example by sending us an email, we receive that information as part of the submission.

On chain, your wallet address is public, and every transaction you make through the Service is a public event readable by anyone with an Abstract RPC connection. We do not link your wallet address to any off-chain identifier unless you choose to connect one to us yourself.

## 3. What we do not collect

We do not run KYC checks against players or holders. We do not require identification documents. We do not sell user data to third parties, and we do not have a data-sales business model that would create incentives to do so.

## 4. Cookies and analytics

The exact analytics provider used by the site will be confirmed before launch. The intent is to use a privacy-respecting provider that reports aggregate traffic patterns without fingerprinting individual visitors, and the specific tool we use will be named in this section once the deployment is finalised.

## 5. Third parties

The Service interacts with several third parties that have access to limited information about your usage. The Abstract network infrastructure, your wallet provider, and any RPC node you use to read or write the chain all see your wallet address. Marketplaces and explorers that render your NFT see the request for the metadata. Vercel (or an equivalent hosting provider) sees the standard server logs for the websites. Each of these third parties operates under its own privacy policy.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. Because the project holds very little personal data off chain, most requests can be resolved quickly. On-chain data is, by its nature, not deletable: the public chain does not support removing past transactions, and we have no ability to do so on your behalf.

## 7. Security

We follow standard practices for the limited amount of off-chain data we hold. No security posture is perfect. If you have reason to believe that any project-controlled system has been compromised, please contact us so we can investigate.

## 8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under the age of 13, or the equivalent age of digital consent in your jurisdiction.

## 9. Changes to this policy

Material changes to this policy will be announced in [/patch-notes](/patch-notes), and the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will move at the same time.

## 10. Contact

A contact email or web form will be added here before launch.
