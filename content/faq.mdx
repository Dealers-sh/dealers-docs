# /faq

The questions we get most.

## Is the art really on chain?

Yes. The SVG character is rendered from packed traits stored on `DealersExeNFT`. The game itself is HTML/JS/CSS, gzipped, stored via `FileStore` on Abstract, and embedded in the NFT's `animation_url`. See [/the-art/rendering](/the-art/rendering).

## What chain is this on?

Abstract. Testnet for development, mainnet at launch. See [/contracts/addresses](/contracts/addresses) for everything.

## Do I need an Abstract Global Wallet?

No, any Abstract-compatible wallet works. AGW is the easiest path because there's no seed phrase. See [/start/connect](/start/connect).

## How do I pronounce it?

"Dealers dot S-H". Not "dealers shell".

## Is this gambling?

It's a strategy game with on-chain stakes. There is no house, no payout pool, no spin-to-win. Outcomes resolve against game logic, not against the contract owner. Read [/contracts/overview](/contracts/overview) and decide for yourself.

## Are you glamorizing drugs?

No. Dealers.sh is stylized 80s arcade fiction, in the lineage of Drug Wars on an MS-DOS machine. The fiction is a game frame. We do not promote real-world drug use.
