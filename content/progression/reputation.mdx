# /progression/reputation

Reputation is the long-running scoring system in Dealers.sh and the single most important measure of your dealer's progress. Every meaningful action in the game adjusts it in one direction or the other, and the total reputation you hold at any moment determines your rank, your access to PVP, and your standing on the global leaderboard.

## How reputation is earned

You earn reputation primarily through the gameplay loops on the deal, PVP, and heist tabs. A clean PVE deal pays a reputation bump scaled to the difficulty of the matchup, the rarity of the drug involved, and the risk you accepted. A successful PVP attack pays a reputation bump scaled to the matchup between you and the target. Heists pay the largest reputation amounts but also carry the largest reputation swings on failure.

A smaller secondary source of reputation comes from achievements. Certain achievements grant a one-time reputation bump on claim, which can be a meaningful boost if you are sitting just below a rank threshold and need a push to cross it. See [/progression/achievements](/progression/achievements) for details on which achievements pay reputation and which pay other kinds of rewards.

## How reputation is lost

The same systems that pay reputation can also charge it. Certain PVE outcomes subtract reputation when the matchup goes badly, particularly when you make repeated weak plays in a row. PVP losses cost reputation as well, and the loss scales with the matchup: losing to a much weaker dealer is more punishing than losing to a stronger one.

Time spent in jail is not, by itself, a direct reputation tax, but it is reputation you are not earning during that period. The opportunity cost is real, and it is part of why heat management is itself a reputation strategy.

The exact reputation curves for each interaction will be published once the contract pass is final.

## What reputation unlocks

Reputation gates several things. The most immediate is rank: your displayed title on the STASH tab is determined entirely by your current reputation total, and crossing a rank threshold updates the label in real time. See [/progression/ranks](/progression/ranks) for the ladder.

Reputation also gates PVP. Until you cross the minimum threshold, the PVP tab is in read-only mode and you cannot commit an attack. Finally, your position on the global leaderboard is primarily a function of reputation, with infamy and other stats acting as tiebreakers.

## Reputation and infamy

It is worth separating reputation from infamy explicitly, because the two are often confused on a first read. Reputation is the slow, broad measure of progress and is earned across all gameplay loops. Infamy is a PVP-specific score that moves faster, swings further, and signals to other dealers how visible you are.

A dealer can have high reputation and low infamy if they have grinded PVE almost exclusively. A dealer can also have moderate reputation and very high infamy if they have run a lot of PVP. Both are legitimate ways to play. They simply produce different reputations, in the conversational sense, around the table.
