## What ranks the board

Primary sort: REP. Tiebreak: Infamy. Second tiebreak: cash on hand.

The board reads on-chain state. There's no separate ledger. If it doesn't match the contract, the contract wins.

## Refresh cadence

The leaderboard view refreshes on a schedule. State changes are immediate on chain; the displayed sort updates at the refresh boundary.

## Why you'd care

- Position on the board may gate certain seasonal achievements.
- Heists and gang formation may use board position as a tiebreaker.
- It looks good on a profile.
