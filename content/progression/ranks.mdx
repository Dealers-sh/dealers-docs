# /progression/ranks

Ranks are the visible expression of your reputation total. As your reputation rises, you cross thresholds that change the title displayed on your STASH tab and in other places where your dealer shows up. The ladder is intentionally familiar to anyone who has seen a mafia movie: it climbs from low-level participants up to the people running the show.

## The ladder

The current outline of the rank ladder is below. The exact reputation thresholds for each tier will be published here once the progression contract is finalised for mainnet.

- Foot Soldier
- Associate
- Soldier
- Capo
- Underboss
- Drug Lord

Each rank covers a band of reputation values, and the displayed title updates the moment your reputation crosses the band boundary. There is no claim step required: ranks are not achievements, they are simply a visualisation of where your reputation total currently sits.

## What ranks affect

Ranks are primarily a status signal, but they also have some functional impact. The visible label on your dealer changes, which is the most immediately noticeable effect. The matchmaking range used to surface PVP targets may take rank into account, so a higher-ranked dealer is more likely to see other higher-ranked dealers in their target list. Some heist eligibility checks may also gate on rank rather than on raw reputation.

What ranks do not do is refund or protect reputation. They are not a streak or a badge that persists once you fall below the threshold. If your reputation drops back below a band boundary, your title drops with it. The number is always the source of truth.
