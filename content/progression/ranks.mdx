The ladder.

Ranks gate behaviors and titles. A higher rank gets a different label on the STASH tab and a different position in PVP target lists.

## The ladder, sketched

- Foot Soldier
- Associate
- Soldier
- Capo
- Underboss
- Drug Lord

The exact REP thresholds will appear here when locked.

## What ranks do

- Affect your visible title.
- May affect PVP matchmaking ranges.
- May affect heist eligibility.

Ranks do not refund REP if you lose it. They are not a streak. They are a level you cross and re-cross. Don't read too much into them. The number is the number.
