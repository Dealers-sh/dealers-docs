# /progression/ranks

The rank ladder is a mafia-themed progression tied to reputation. New dealers start near the bottom of the ladder — Foot Soldier territory — and climb as they accumulate REP through PVE and PVP. Each rank shifts how the game and other players talk about your dealer: the title on your STASH tab updates the moment your reputation crosses a band boundary, and the change is purely a visualisation of where your total currently sits. There is no claim step.

## What ranks affect

Ranks are primarily a status signal, but they also have functional impact. PVP unlocks at a reputation threshold, so until you cross it the PVP tab is read-only. Certain pager subjects and area interactions may also gate by rank rather than by raw reputation. If your reputation drops back below a band boundary, your title drops with it — the number is always the source of truth.

## Live thresholds are in-game

The docs do not maintain a rank-threshold table. From the 📟 tab, type `/info dealer` to see your current rank and the next threshold, or `/rank` to see your standing in the overall rankings.
