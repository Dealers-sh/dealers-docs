# /progression/achievements

Things you can do that the contract notices.

## How they work

Some achievements are auto-applied as you play. Others sit in your [/the-game/pager](/the-game/pager) waiting for you to claim them.

Claiming is a transaction. The achievement contract verifies you earned it. The reward applies on confirm.

Categories will include:

- **First-deal milestones.** Welcome gifts.
- **Streak achievements.** N deals without jail. N PVP wins in a row.
- **Heat achievements.** Survive a 5-heat run without jail. Clear heat N times.
- **PVP achievements.** First clip. First clean steal of $X. Best-in-area.
- **Heist achievements.** First job. First clean job. Loot thresholds.
- **Time achievements.** Active days, period streaks.

## What rewards look like

Rewards vary by achievement:

- Cash drops.
- REP bumps.
- Stash drops (specific drugs).
- Visual NFT trait changes that show on the character.

The last one is the fun one. Your dealer levels up. Your JPEG doesn't.
