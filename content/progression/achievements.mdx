# /progression/achievements

An achievement in Dealers.sh is an on-chain record of something specific your dealer has done. Some achievements apply automatically as soon as you meet their criteria. Others require an explicit on-chain claim, which lets you choose the moment of confirmation and pay the gas yourself. The system is implemented in the `DealersClaims` contract.

## How a claim works

To check your achievements, open the 📟 [Pager](/the-game/pager) and type `/achievements`. The pager renders a list of every achievement with its current status: `CLAIMED`, `CLAIM` (criteria met, ready to claim), or `VIEW` (locked). Tapping `CLAIM` opens a modal that summarises the reward; signing the transaction calls the claims contract, which verifies the criteria on chain and settles the reward to your dealer. Because verification happens on chain, claims cannot be faked.

## What the rewards look like

Rewards vary by achievement. Some grant a flat cash drop. Some grant a reputation bump that can push you across a rank threshold. Some grant a specific drug or set of drugs to your stash. 

## The full catalogue is in-game

The docs do not duplicate the achievement list. From the 📟 tab, type `/achievements` to see every achievement, what it requires, and the reward it pays.
