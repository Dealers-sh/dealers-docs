# /the-game/jail

Not where you wanted to be.

## How you got here

Heat at 5 puts you in jail. Heat at 5 comes from:

- A run of failed deals that you didn't clear.
- One catastrophic deal.
- Losing PVP fights at a high enough level.

## What jail does

While in jail:

- 🤝 is off. No deals.
- ⚔️ is off. No PVP.
- 🏦 is off. No heists.
- ✈️ shows two new actions: **bail** and **breakout**.

## Bail

- **Cost:** cash. Scales with your status.
- **Outcome:** guaranteed release.
- **When to use it:** when you have the cash and you have plans tonight.

## Breakout

- **Cost:** zero cash if it works. More time if it doesn't.
- **Outcome:** rolled. You either walk free or you stay in and the meter resets.
- **When to use it:** when you're cash-poor or feeling lucky.

## After release

You come out with Heat at 0 and a fresh start on the streets. REP and stash are preserved. Cash spent on bail is gone.

Don't speedrun back to 5.
