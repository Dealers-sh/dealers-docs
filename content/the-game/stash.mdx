# /the-game/stash

The STASH tab is your dashboard. It's the default view. Always available.

## What it shows

- **Left panel.** Your stats: REP, Heat, Cash, Attempts. Plus rank label and area.
- **Right panel.** Your drug inventory. Type, amount, and weight.

## What you do here

Nothing, directly. STASH is for reading, not for acting. From here you tap into the action tabs:

- 🤝 [/the-game/deal](/the-game/deal)
- ✈️ [/the-game/move](/the-game/move)
- ⚔️ [/the-game/pvp](/the-game/pvp)
- 🏦 [/the-game/heist](/the-game/heist)
- 🛍️ [/the-game/shop](/the-game/shop)
- 📟 [/the-game/pager](/the-game/pager)

## Reading the numbers

| Line | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `REP nnn` | Reputation total. |
| `🔥 n/5` | Current heat level. |
| `$nnn,nnn` | Cash on hand. |
| `⚡️ n/N` | Attempts remaining this period. |
| `Area: <name>` | Where you are. Affects everything else. |
