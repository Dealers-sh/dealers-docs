# /the-game/shop

The 🛍️ tab is where you convert cash into edges. The shop sells two broad categories of items: time-limited boosts that multiply your gains for a fixed window, and consumables like attempt resets that let you keep playing when you would otherwise have to wait for the daily timer.

## The boosts

There are three tiers of boost, each with its own price, duration, and multiplier. Grinder is the entry-level option: it is the cheapest, has the shortest active window, and provides a modest multiplier on certain gains. Hustler is the mid-tier choice and is the boost most active players reach for during a regular session. Kingpin is the top tier, with the highest cost, the longest window, and the largest multiplier.

The exact percentages and durations for each tier will be published here once the boost contract pass is final.

## When boosts are worth it

A boost multiplies whatever you do during its active window, which means it is most valuable when you are already on a strategy you know is profitable. The classic use case is a planned arbitrage run: you have identified a price gap between two areas, you have the cash to move significant inventory, and you want to extract maximum value before the window closes. Stacking a Hustler or Kingpin on top of that run is almost always the right call.

The opposite is also true. Burning a Kingpin on a single speculative PVP attack, or on a deal where you are not sure of the matchup, is the fastest way to waste a boost. If you would not bet on the underlying play without the boost, the boost itself does not change the odds. It only changes the size of the outcome.

## Attempt resets

The shop also sells attempt resets, which top your daily attempts back up to the maximum. When you have run out of attempts and your daily refill is still hours away, a reset lets you keep playing immediately. The reset is the right purchase when you are mid-run and the next deal is worth more than the reset price. It is the wrong purchase when you are bored and looking to do something just to do something.
