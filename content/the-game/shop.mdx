# /the-game/shop

The 🛍️ tab. Spend cash on edges.

## What's for sale

The shop carries time-limited boosts and consumables. Three boost tiers:

- **Grinder.** Entry-level multiplier. Cheapest. Shortest window.
- **Hustler.** Mid-tier. The workhorse boost.
- **Kingpin.** Top tier. Most expensive. Biggest multiplier.

Also available:

- **Attempt resets.** When you've burned through your period's attempts and don't want to wait for the daily reset.

## When to use a boost

Boosts multiply gains for a window. They are not magic. Stack them with a run that already has a strategy, not with a coin flip.

A good rule: boost on a profitable area + boost on a known-cheap stash run = best return. Wasting a Kingpin on a random PVP attack is how you ruin a Friday.
