# /the-game/drugs-and-areas

The reference table.

## Drugs

Each drug has its own rarity and its own price band. Common drugs are widely available across areas. Rare drugs only appear in specific spots.

## Areas

Each area has:

- A name (Manhattan and others).
- A type: **streets**, **safe house**, **black market**, **jail**.
- A drug list available for buying and selling.
- A local price band that swings around a base value.

## The pattern

Where you'd expect a price drop, prices drop. Specific drugs cluster in specific neighborhoods. The arbitrage isn't hidden. You just have to remember it.
