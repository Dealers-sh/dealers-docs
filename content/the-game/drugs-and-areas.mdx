# /the-game/drugs-and-areas

This page is the reference table that the rest of the game relies on. Every drug in Dealers.sh has its own rarity tier and its own price band, and every area has its own subset of drugs available for trade. The combination of those two facts is what makes price arbitrage possible.

## Drugs

The drug catalogue is maintained on chain in the `DEDrugRegistry` contract. Each drug has a name, a rarity tier, and a base price that the local prices fluctuate around. Common drugs tend to be available across many areas, which keeps their prices relatively similar from place to place. Rarer drugs appear in fewer areas, which means the gap between the lowest and highest local price is wider and the arbitrage opportunity is larger.

The final drug list and rarity tiers will be published here as a table once the registry is locked for mainnet. Until then, the in-game UI is the source of truth: it reads the registry directly and reflects whatever is currently deployed.

## Areas

The area catalogue is maintained in the `DEAreaRegistry` contract and follows the same structure. Each area has a name, a type (streets, safe house, black market, or jail), a list of available drugs, and a price band that swings around a base value. Some areas trade in a wide selection of common drugs, and others specialise in a narrower set of rarer drugs.

As with drugs, the final area list will be published here in table form once the registry is finalised for mainnet.

## How prices move

Local prices are not static. Each area's price for a given drug fluctuates within its band, and the fluctuations are part of what drives the moment-to-moment decisions you make as a player. A drug you bought yesterday at a low price might be even cheaper today, which means it is worth holding, or it might be approaching the top of its band, which means it is time to sell or to move on.

The pattern is not hidden. Areas that look like they should have cheap supply tend to have cheap supply, and areas that look like they should pay a premium tend to pay a premium. The fiction matches the economics on purpose, and learning the geography is part of getting good at the game.

When the registries are final, this page will become the canonical lookup table for both halves of the system.
