# /the-game/heist

The 🏦 tab is where the largest single payouts in Dealers.sh live. A heist is a coordinated, multi-step job that pays more than any individual deal or PVP attack, but also carries more setup, more downside, and longer cooldowns. The full mechanics are being finalised in the final contract pass before launch, so this page documents what we can confirm today and reserves the rest for the launch update.

## What we can confirm

Heists are not a single instantaneous action. They unfold across several steps, each of which has its own decisions and its own resolution. A failed heist costs more than a failed deal, and a successful heist pays more than any successful deal, which is why the game gates them behind progression and resources rather than offering them to a fresh dealer on day one.

The intent of the system is to give experienced players a reason to keep playing after they have outgrown the standard PVE loop. By the time you are pulling regular heists, your dealer has graduated from the streets into something closer to organised crime, and the rhythm of play changes accordingly.

## What is still to come

When the heist mechanics finalise, this page will be expanded to cover the full setup flow, any crew or coordination requirements, the gear and stake involved, the outcome and resolution logic, the loot table, and the cooldown and heat impact. Until then, the community channels are the best place to ask about the current state.

The high-level positioning we use is: PVE for the rep, PVP for the infamy, heists for the bag.
