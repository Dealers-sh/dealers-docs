# /the-game/bank

The 🏦 tab shows a single line:

> You overheard a banker mentioning their vault containing ~`X` ETH.

`X` is the live balance of the bank vault, read on chain at render time. There is nothing to click. The vault accumulates a share of the fees flowing through the game, and the tab is a hint at a future game mode built on top of it — a community bank heist event. The daily push-your-luck runs on the [🚚 Heists tab](/the-game/heists) are already live; the bank vault event is not playable yet.
