# /the-game/move

The ✈️ tab is what makes the rest of the game work. Every area in Dealers.sh has its own price list and its own selection of available drugs, which means the only way to profit from price differences is to travel between them. Without movement, there is no arbitrage, and without arbitrage there is no game.

## Why you would move

There are several distinct reasons to relocate, and most experienced players cycle through all of them in a session. The most common reason is price arbitrage: you have bought stock in one area and want to sell it where the local price is higher. The second reason is heat management: a Safe House lets you sit out for a stretch and cool down without spending cash on the Clear Heat action. The third reason is PVP: a different area means a different list of potential targets, which is useful if your current area's pool of dealers has gone stale or hostile.

## Types of areas

Most areas you encounter are normal streets, which means full access to the deal tab, the PVP tab, and travel. A few special types of areas behave differently.

A Safe House blocks deals and PVP, and it generally exists to let you lay low while heat dissipates. A Black Market replaces the standard deal menu with a different vendor's price list, which is useful for selling at a better price on certain drugs. A Jail location is reached only when your heat reaches 5, and the rules inside are documented separately on [/the-game/jail](/the-game/jail).

## The move flow

Travel is straightforward. Open the ✈️ tab, swipe through the list of nearby areas, and you will see each area's name, type, and current set of available drugs. When you find a destination you like, hit confirm. The move costs cash and consumes one attempt, and the transaction is mined on chain like any other action. As soon as you land, the price list and target pool update to match your new location.

## When the move tab handles jail

When your heat hits 5, the game moves you to jail automatically, and the move tab takes on a different role. Instead of choosing a destination, you choose how to leave. Paying bail costs cash but is guaranteed to release you. Attempting a breakout costs no cash but is not guaranteed: it can fail and leave you locked in for another period.

For the full mechanics of both options, see [/the-game/jail](/the-game/jail).
