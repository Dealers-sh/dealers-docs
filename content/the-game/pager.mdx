# /the-game/pager

The 📟 tab is the inbox for your dealer. It collects notifications from the game itself, alerts about events involving other players, and any achievements you have earned but not yet claimed. Most players check the pager at the start of a session to catch up on what happened while they were away.

## What appears in the pager

System notifications cover the routine background events: when a new period rolls over, when your daily attempts refill, when a jail sentence completes. PVP notifications cover anything involving other dealers: who attacked you, how the fight resolved, and when a dealer you previously scouted comes back into your area. Family announcements appear when something ships at the project level that affects how holders play. Finally, claimable achievements show up here when you have earned one but have not yet completed the on-chain claim transaction.

## Claiming achievements

Some achievements apply automatically as soon as you meet their criteria. Others require an on-chain claim transaction before the reward applies. The pager is where the second category lives. Each unclaimed achievement appears with a button that opens a confirmation flow, and confirming the transaction settles the reward to your dealer.

The reward varies depending on the achievement. Some grant a one-time cash drop, some grant a reputation bump, some add a specific drug to your stash, and some unlock a visual trait change that becomes part of your dealer's appearance. The full list of achievements and their effects will be documented on [/progression/achievements](/progression/achievements) as the catalog is finalised.
