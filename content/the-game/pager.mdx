# /the-game/pager

The 📟 tab. Your inbox.

## What lands here

- **System messages.** Period rolls, daily resets, jail releases.
- **PVP notifications.** Someone clipped you. Someone's been spying. You moved into someone's range.
- **Achievements.** Unlocked but not yet claimed. Claim from this tab.
- **Family announcements.** When we ship something on chain that affects you, this is where you read about it first.

## Claiming achievements

Some achievements are auto-applied. Others wait for you to claim them. Claimable ones show up here with a button.

A claim is a transaction. It costs gas. The reward is whatever the achievement says: a reputation bump, an NFT trait state, a stash drop, depending.

See [/progression/achievements](/progression/achievements) for the full list and what each does.
