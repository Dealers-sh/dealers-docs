# /the-game/pvp

The ⚔️ tab. Other dealers are in your area. Take what they've got.

## Unlocking PVP

PVP is rep-gated. You need a minimum reputation to attack. Until you hit the threshold, the tab is read-only. You can scout, but not strike.

PVP is also off when:

- You're in **Jail**.
- You're in a **Safe House**.

## The flow

1. **Browse targets.** The list shows dealers in your current area worth hitting. They are real holders, in real time.
2. **Spy.** Tap a target to inspect them. You see what they're carrying and what kind of fight you're picking.
3. **Attack.** Commit. The attack resolves on chain.
4. **Result.** Win and you take some cash and a drug. Lose and you walk away empty. Either way, REP and Infamy move.

## What you win

A clean hit pays:

- **Cash stolen** from the target's holdings.
- **One drug type** at a quantity rolled at resolution.
- **+REP**, scaled to the matchup.
- **+Infamy.** PVP is how other dealers learn your name.

## What you lose

A miss or a fumble costs you:

- **REP** drops. Sometimes by a lot.
- **Infamy** can move negative. Losing PVP fights makes you look soft.
- **Heat** can rise. The cops do not love a street fight.

## Why bother

REP is the slow grind. Infamy is the fast lane. PVE for the rep. PVP for the infamy.

A dealer with high Infamy is a target everyone wants to clip. That's the trade. Visibility for reward.

## Etiquette

You can't be revenge-baited into anything. Targeting is your call, not theirs. If someone clips you, they go into your spy stash for later. The pager will say.
